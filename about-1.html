Our Company

Our Expertise Stands in
Import-Export Logistics

At Swift Resources, import-export is more than moving goods from one place to another — it's about delivering trust, reliability, and efficiency. With 12 years of experience, we support Thai and international traders with customs clearance, freight, warehousing, and door-to-door delivery.

Leadership

Kailash Shrestha

Director, Swift Resources Co., Ltd. — Bangkok

Kailash Shrestha is the Director of Swift Resources and a renowned exporter from Bangkok specializing in cosmetics, electronics, food grains, and garments. He helps clients find the right suppliers, secure quality goods, and deliver shipments reliably across the world.

  • Global Service

    We move garments, cosmetics, seafood, and other cargo to and from global markets — with reliable freight booking and full customs documentation support.

  • Local Service

    Across Thailand we handle warehousing, inland transport, and last-mile delivery for fashion, beauty, cold-chain seafood, and everyday commercial goods.
