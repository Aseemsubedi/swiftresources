FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

What documents are required for import-export shipments?

We prepare and manage commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, bills of lading, and customs declarations so your shipment meets Thai and international trade requirements.

How long does customs clearance take?

Most routine clearances are completed within 1–3 business days, depending on cargo type, inspection requirements, and document readiness. Urgent shipments can be prioritized with advance notice.

What happens if my shipment is delayed or lost?

We track every shipment closely and notify you immediately of any delay. Our team works with carriers and customs to resolve issues quickly, and cargo insurance options are available for added protection.

How can I reduce my import-export shipping costs?

Choosing the right freight mode, consolidating cargo, preparing accurate documents, and planning ahead can lower costs. Share your shipment details and we will recommend the most cost-effective route.
