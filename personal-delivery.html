What documents are required for import-export shipments?

Last-mile deliveries are supported by delivery orders, commercial invoices, and any permits required for restricted goods. We verify documentation before dispatch to ensure compliant handover at the final destination.

How long does customs clearance take?

For domestic distribution of cleared goods, delivery timing depends on route and volume rather than customs processing. Import cargo is delivered only after release, and we coordinate pickup from port or warehouse accordingly.

What happens if my shipment is delayed or lost?

If a delivery window is missed, our dispatch team re-routes the driver or arranges a replacement run. GPS tracking and driver contact allow us to resolve issues quickly and keep recipients informed.

How can I reduce my import-export shipping costs?

Batching deliveries by region, optimizing route density, and aligning dispatch with warehouse cut-off times reduce per-unit transport cost. We review your delivery patterns and recommend schedules that balance speed and spend.
