What Is Our Privacy Policy?

At Swift Resources Co., Ltd., we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect data when you interact with our services, website, or contact us for quotes and inquiries. We comply with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and respect your privacy rights.

  • We collect contact information from website forms and WhatsApp inquiries.

  • Your data is used for service quotes, shipment tracking, and communication.

  • We share information only with logistics partners when necessary.

  • Cookies improve your experience on our website.

  • We retain your data only as long as needed for our services.

  • You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, and company details when you request a quote, make an inquiry through our contact forms, or communicate with us via WhatsApp or email. We also collect shipment details such as cargo descriptions, destinations, and documentation required for customs clearance and freight services.

Additionally, our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience. These may include session cookies, analytics cookies, and functional cookies that help us understand how visitors interact with our site. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to provide import-export logistics services, including preparing quotes, coordinating shipments, managing customs clearance, and delivering your cargo. Your information helps us communicate with you about your shipment status, respond to inquiries, and improve our services. We may also use aggregated, non-identifiable data for internal analysis and service improvement.

  • Providing quotes and coordinating import-export shipments.

  • Communicating with you about your shipment and service updates.

  • Complying with legal requirements and customs regulations.

  • Improving our logistics services based on customer feedback.

Data Sharing and Security

We share your information only when necessary to fulfill our logistics services. This includes sharing with shipping carriers, customs authorities, warehouse partners, and other third-party service providers involved in your shipment. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no electronic transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  • Data shared with logistics partners for service delivery.

  • Information disclosed to customs and regulatory authorities as required.

  • Security measures in place to protect your personal data.

  • No sale or rent of your personal information to third parties.

  • Data retention period based on service and legal requirements.

  • Your right to access and request correction of your data.

  • Right to request deletion of your personal information.

  • Compliance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at swiftresourcesltd@gmail.com or +66 081 478 5100. You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. We will respond to your request in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Swift Resources Co., Ltd. reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.
